$$pH=-log[H^+]$$
$$pOH=-\log[OH^-]$$
$$pH=14-pOH$$
#### Information:
- pH = power of hidrogen
- pOH = power of hydroxide
- $H^+$= concentration of $H^+$
- $OH^-$ = concentration of $OH^-$
