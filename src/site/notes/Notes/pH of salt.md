#### Acid
$$[H^+]=\sqrt{\frac{K_w}{K_b}×[salt]}$$
#### Base
$$[OH^-]=\sqrt{\frac{K_w}{K_a}×[salt]}$$
#### Neutral / Acid-Base
$$[H^+]=\sqrt{\frac{K_w}{K_b}×[K_a]}$$
