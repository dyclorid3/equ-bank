#### sinuses
$$\sin\frac{1}{2}\alpha=\sqrt{\frac{1-(\cos\alpha)}{2}}$$
#### cosine
$$\cos\frac{1}{2}\alpha=\sqrt{\frac{1+(\cos\alpha)}{2}}$$
#### tangent
$$\tan\frac{1}{2}\alpha=\sqrt{\frac{1-(\cos\alpha)}{1+(\cos\alpha)}}$$
