#### sinuses
$$\sin\alpha=\sqrt{\frac{1-(\cos\frac{1}{2}\alpha)}{2}}$$
#### cosine
$$\cos\alpha=\sqrt{\frac{1+(\cos\frac{1}{2}\alpha)}{2}}$$
#### tangent
$$\tan\alpha=\sqrt{\frac{1-(\cos\frac{1}{2}\alpha)}{1+(\cos\frac{1}{2}\alpha)}}$$
