#### Acid
$$[H^+]=K_a×\frac{nHA}{nA^-}$$
#### Base
$$[OH^-]=K_b×\frac{BOH}{nB^+}$$
#### Information
- nHA = mole of acid
- nBOH = mole of base
- n$A^-$ = mole of conjugate base
- n$B^+$ = mole of conjugate acid
- $K_a$ = acid ionization constant
- $K_b$ = base ionization constant
