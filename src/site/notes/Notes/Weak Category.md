#### Acid
$$[H^+]=\sqrt{[HA]×K_a}$$
#### Base
$$[OH^-]=\sqrt{[BOH]×K_b}$$
#### Information
- $[HA]$ = concentration of $[HA]$
- $[BOH]$ = concentration of $[BOH]$
- $K_a$ = Acid ionization constant
- $K_b$ = Base ionization constant
